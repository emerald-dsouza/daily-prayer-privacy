Daily Prayer Companion

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Daily Prayer Companion is a Catholic prayer app for Android phones and Wear OS watches. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is handled. The short version is: the app collects no personal data, sends nothing over the internet, and keeps everything you enter on your own device.

At a glance
  • No account, no sign-in, no profile you create with us.
  • No data collection, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or third-party SDKs.
  • Works fully offline; no internet connection is used.
  • Everything you enter is stored only on your device and your paired Wear OS watch.

1. What we collect

We collect nothing. The app does not collect, transmit, or receive any personal information. It performs no network requests of any kind. There are no servers, no databases, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party advertising or tracking frameworks in the app.

2. Data that stays on your device

Some information is stored locally on your device so the app can remember your preferences and progress between sessions. This includes:

  • Your focus profile (the occasions, moods, and themes you choose to guide recommendations);
  • Your prayer-ring progress and streaks;
  • Your private journal of "notices" — reflections you record while praying;
  • Your progress through the daily Scripture readings;
  • Prayers you have favourited and the prayer you pin to your Home screen;
  • Your reminder and quiet-hours settings.

All of this is stored on your device using Android's protected storage. It never leaves your device, is never uploaded, and is never accessible to us or to any other party.

3. Your Wear OS companion

The optional Wear OS watch app keeps a copy of this device-local data to show your prayer ring and a gentle reminder on your wrist. That copy is transferred directly between your phone and your watch over the Wear OS Data Layer — a private, device-to-device link. It is not transmitted over the internet and never passes through our servers (we have none).

4. Notifications

If you enable "gentle reminders," the app schedules local notifications that are delivered by Android on your device. They are generated and stored entirely on your device; nothing about them is sent to us. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in Android system settings. The app asks for notification permission only after you choose to set a reminder — never at first launch.

5. How your information is used and shared

Because the app collects no information, there is nothing to use or share. Specifically:

  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with any third party.
  • No data is used for advertising or advertising measurement.
  • No data is used for tracking across apps or websites.
  • No data is used to build any profile of you outside your own device.

6. Security

Your device-local data is protected by the standard security and file protection provided by Android and Wear OS. The app contains no networking code, so there is no attack surface for transmitting your data. Because all information lives only on your device, the strongest protection you have is your device's own lock screen and passcode.

7. Deleting your data

To delete all data the app stores, simply delete the app from your phone (and your watch). All locally stored data — your focus, rings, journal, favourites, reading progress, and reminder settings — is removed with it. We hold no copy anywhere, because we never receive your data in the first place.

8. Children's privacy

The app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected, there is nothing to disclose about how children's information is handled.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date. Because the app's privacy posture — no collection, no transmission — is a property of the app itself, any future change that would affect that posture would also be described clearly here.
